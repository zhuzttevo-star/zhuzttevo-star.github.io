Riot Town (formerly Restaurant Riot) — Support, Rules & Privacy

A multiplayer restaurant sandbox for iPhone by Andrii Motruk.

Contact & reports

Questions, bugs, or a player who broke the rules? Email zhuzttevo@gmail.com. Reports about abusive players are reviewed within 24 hours. In the game you can also open the menu (II button) → PLAYERS → REPORT: the player is blocked at once and an email with the report is prepared for you.

How to play

  • RANDOM finds players through Game Center. When the lobby is ready, a roulette wheel gives everyone a character. Free characters are played by bots, so a game always starts.
  • PLAY WITH FRIENDS opens the Game Center invite screen. In the friends lobby the leader chooses CLOSED SESSION (everyone picks a character, free ones are bots) or RANDOM (more random players join and the roulette decides). The game starts when everybody taps READY.
  • Left side of the screen — move (push far to run). Right side — look around. Buttons: PUNCH / USE, KICK, JUMP, PICK UP / DROP (hold to throw), CROUCH, DANCE. The yellow button shows what you can do right here: chop, pour, drink, eat, sit, serve, draw on the board and more.
  • Cook: chop vegetables on a cutting board (swipe down), fry patties and steaks in a pan on the stove, plate the food and serve the guests. Bar: pour bottles into glasses or the shaker, shake, pour a cocktail.
  • Grab a marker and walk up to the big board in the dining hall to draw. Tap MIC to talk to other players.

House rules

Riot Town is a cartoon game. Be kind to other players: no hate speech, harassment, threats, sexual content or sharing personal information in voice chat or drawings. Players who break the rules can be muted, blocked and reported. Blocked players' voices are silenced and their drawings are hidden on your device.

Privacy policy

  • The game has no accounts of its own and no analytics. The developer does not collect, store or sell any personal data.
  • Ads. During a game a small banner ad is shown at the top of the screen. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may use your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow it in the "Allow tracking" window — you can change this any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking), your IP address and basic device information. In the EU/UK you are asked for consent first. See how Google uses data in ads and Google's privacy policy.
  • No Ads purchase. "Remove Ads" is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple. The developer never sees your payment details; the game only remembers on your iPhone that ads are turned off. Use SETTINGS → RESTORE PURCHASE to get it back on a new iPhone.
  • Multiplayer works through Apple Game Center. Your Game Center nickname is shown to other players in the game (on your character's name badge). Apple's privacy policy applies to Game Center.
  • Microphone: used only for voice chat, and only after you tap MIC. Your voice is sent in real time to the other players in your game through Game Center and is not recorded or stored by the developer.
  • Drawings on the in-game board are sent to the other players in the same game and disappear when the game ends.
  • Settings, the list of muted / blocked players and your reports are stored only on your iPhone.
  • If you send a report by email, the developer receives only what that email contains and uses it only to handle the report.
  • The game is not directed at children.
