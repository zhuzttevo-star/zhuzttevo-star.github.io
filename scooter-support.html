Scooter Scout Rush — Support

An endless scooter runner for iPhone by Andrii Motruk.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email zhuzttevo@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something does not work. I usually reply within a few days.

How to play

  • Swipe left / right — change lanes.
  • Swipe up — jump with a trick. Tap in the air — Extra Spin.
  • Swipe down — duck under scaffolding.
  • Collect Likes, pull off tricks for Style Points and don't let Granny catch you!

FAQ

Do I need an account or the internet?
No. The game works offline. An internet connection is only needed for optional reward ads.

Are ads required?
No. Ads only play when you tap a button that offers a reward (a second chance, double Likes or a free scooter).

My progress is gone.
Progress is saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the game deletes the progress.

How do I change ad tracking?
iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
