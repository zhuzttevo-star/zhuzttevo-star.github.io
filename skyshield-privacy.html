Privacy Policy

App: Sky Shield: Air Defense · Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game Sky Shield (the "App") processes and why.

Who we are

Developer: Andrii Motruk
Contact e-mail: zhuzttevo@gmail.com

Data we collect ourselves

The App does not collect or send any personal data to us. It does not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Your game progress (coins, levels, stars, weapons, settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Data processed by third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • the device advertising ID;
  • approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • technical information such as device model, operating system version and diagnostics;
  • interactions with ads.

Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google Play Billing (purchases)

If you buy Premium, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. The App never receives your card or payment details — it only learns that the purchase was made.

Consent for personalised ads

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a consent form before any ads are requested. You can refuse personalised ads — you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised. You can change your choice at any time with the PRIVACY button in the App's main menu.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at the e-mail above and we will delete it.

Data retention and deletion

We do not store any of your data on our side, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete. Data processed by Google is handled under Google's policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
