Pirate Clash: Sea Duel — Support & Privacy

Naval battles for iPhone by Andrii Motruk.

How to play

  • Choose a battle mode with the button above PLAY: Duel (1 vs 1), Fleet Battle (3 vs 3), Free for All (6 ships), Capture the Base (6 vs 6) or Super Storm (five crews of two).
  • Tap PLAY to find captains through Game Center. Empty seats are filled with AI captains, so a battle always starts. WITH FRIENDS opens the Game Center invite screen.
  • Duel: drag to aim your cannons and tap FIRE for a full broadside.
  • Sea modes: steer with the left joystick (forward sets the sails, sideways turns the ship), swipe to look around, FIRE shoots the broadside you are looking at and AIM switches to the cannon view.
  • Capture the Base: hold the enemy flag zone for 30 seconds or sink the whole enemy fleet.
  • Super Storm: stay inside the shrinking eye of the storm. One pearl chest surfaces somewhere on the map; sail into it to grab 10 pearls.
  • Chain shot topples masts, fire shot sets ships ablaze, explosive shells deal huge damage. Pick your 4 ammo types in the ARMORY.
  • Upgrade your ships, unlock new ones and decorate them in the harbor.

Purchases

Pearls (the premium currency) and Captain's Charter can be bought with real money through the App Store. Captain's Charter removes ad breaks between battles; ads you choose to watch for rewards stay available. To restore Captain's Charter on a new device: Settings (gear icon) → RESTORE PURCHASES. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Pirate Clash: Sea Duel does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

  • Game progress (ships, upgrades, currencies, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • Online battles use Apple Game Center. Your Game Center nickname and the ship you sail are shown to the other captains in your battle. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. The advertising identifier is used for personalised ads only if you allow tracking when the game asks. In the EEA/UK you can change your consent in the consent form. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

If you have questions about this policy or want to ask for data deletion, write to zhuzttevo@gmail.com.
