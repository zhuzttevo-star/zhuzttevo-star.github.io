Scooter Scout Rush — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Scooter Scout Rush (“the game”) is an iPhone game made by Andrii Motruk, an independent developer (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what information is used when you play the game and why.

Short version

  • The game has no accounts, no registration and no chat. I do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress (scores, likes, scooters, outfits, settings) is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows optional reward ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • You decide whether ads may be personalized: through Apple’s “Allow tracking” prompt and, in Europe, the Google consent message.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings in a file on your iPhone. This file is not sent to me or to anyone else. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob to show rewarded video ads that you choose to watch (for example, to get a second chance or a reward). To show, measure and protect these ads from fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking in Apple’s prompt;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device information (model, operating system version, language);
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown, viewed or tapped);
  • diagnostic data about ad loading and app performance.

I do not receive this information in a form that identifies you; I only see summary reports from AdMob. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Tracking on iPhone. When the game first starts, iOS asks whether the game may track you. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, ads are not personalized using your Advertising Identifier. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Europe, UK and Switzerland. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a Google consent message lets you accept or refuse the use of your data for ads. Refusing still lets you play; you may see non-personalized or limited ads.
  • Playing without ads. All ads in the game are optional — you only see one when you tap a button that offers a reward for watching it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG). If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact me and I will help remove it.

Data retention and your rights

I do not store personal data about you on any server. Data collected by Google for advertising is handled under Google’s policies. Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. For advertising data, you can use the choices above or contact Google. For any question about this game, contact me.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it uses data (for example, if online leaderboards are added), I will update this page and change the effective date above.
